# Venus Phase Calendar (金星満ち欠けカレンダー)
[日本語](./README.md)

[**🌐 Open Venus Phase Calendar**](https://gambaranai.github.io/Venus-Calendar/)

**Venus Phase Calendar** is a high-precision astronomical simulation app that lets you interactively explore Venus's phases, apparent size, and distance from Earth — in real time, or for any date in the past or future.

## 🌟 Features

- **Venus Phase Simulation**  
  Visualizes how Venus appears on any selected date — crescent, half, gibbous, or full — just as you would see it through a telescope. Easily identify when Venus is the Morning Star or Evening Star.

- **Orbital Model (Sun · Earth · Venus)**  
  An interactive orbital diagram shows the relative positions of Earth and Venus around the Sun, making it easy to understand *why* Venus goes through phases.

- **Precise Astronomical Data**  
  Automatically calculates illumination percentage, apparent magnitude, distance from Earth (in Astronomical Units: AU), and angular diameter for any date.

- **Calendar & Event Search**  
  Navigate dates freely with a calendar interface. Quickly search for key astronomical events such as greatest elongation, inferior conjunction, and superior conjunction.

## 🔍 Why We Built This

Venus is one of the brightest objects in the night sky. Through binoculars or a small telescope, you can observe its phases and dramatic changes in apparent size — just as Galileo did four centuries ago.

The **Venus Phase Calendar** was built for:
- Amateur astronomers planning observation sessions
- Anyone curious about space and the night sky
- Students and educators exploring planetary astronomy

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript (Vanilla)
- [Astronomy Engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy) — high-precision planetary position calculations
- GitHub Pages — hosting

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the app via the link above.
2. Select a date on the calendar, or press **"Today"** to jump to the current date.
3. The central Venus graphic shows the planet's illuminated phase for that day.
4. Press **"🪐 Orbital Model"** to view the orbital positions of Earth and Venus relative to the Sun.
5. The bottom panel displays detailed astronomical data and upcoming major events (e.g. greatest brilliancy).
